Cabin rules

  • Every sound can be switched off and has a caption.
  • Cabin ambience is off by default; nothing plays until you tap.
  • Reduce motion: follows the system setting and an in-app switch.
  • Works with a keyboard and a screen reader; the timer is not announced every second, there is a "read remaining time" button.
  • Light, dark or automatic theme. The flight screen is always the night cabin.

Questions

Do I need internet?

No. The airport list, drawn map, fonts and sounds come with the app; after the first load it works offline. The satellite map in the phone app needs internet; without a connection it switches to the drawn map.

If my phone locks, does the timer drift?

No. The timer is calculated from the start time, so the remaining time is correct with the screen locked or the app in the background. Cabin sound can keep playing while locked (Settings › Cabin › “Keep sound playing when locked”); on iPhone in the browser this is not always guaranteed.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, login or email.

Where is my data?

On your device. We cannot see it. You can export or delete it from Settings. When you change devices, export and import.

Is it an official app for YKS, LGS or KPSS?

No. Albatros is not affiliated with OSYM or MEB. The exam names only say which exam you are preparing for. It promises no exam result.

Does it cost anything?

Not now. Time, route and record options will not be paid features.

What if I stop early?

Divert: you land at the nearest airport. The minutes you studied stay in your logbook. No penalties, and rest days don't count against your goal.
