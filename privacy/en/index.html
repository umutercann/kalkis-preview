Albatros Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short

  • Albatros does not ask for an account, email, phone number or location. The name printed on your boarding pass is optional and stays only on your device.
  • Your data (flights, settings, notes) stays on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • There is no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library.
  • The app does not send your flights, notes or name to us or to any other server.
  • The satellite map in the phone apps downloads map imagery from Apple (iOS) or Google (Android). While doing so, the map provider receives device and usage information (see section 4). Without internet, the app uses the drawn map stored on your device.
  • "Erase all my data" removes everything on your device in one step.

1. Who is responsible

Developer of Albatros: Umut Ercan

Because the app's data stays on your device, we cannot access it. This policy still explains what is processed, where and why, to meet the transparency duties of the GDPR (Art. 13) and Turkey's KVKK (Law No. 6698, Art. 10).

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Purpose: to provide the timer, logbook, passport and goal.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) (providing the service you asked for) and 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests). KVKK Art. 5/2-(c) and 5/2-(f). No processing relies on consent.
  • Storage on your device is strictly necessary for the service you explicitly request, so no cookie or tracking banner is shown. The app uses no cookies and does not track you.

4. Sharing with third parties

Your flights, notes, settings and name are not sent to us or to anyone else. The only data that leaves your device is:

Map (Android and iOS apps). Satellite and standard map imagery is loaded from the internet:

  • iOS: Apple Maps (MapKit). Map tiles are downloaded from Apple Inc. (USA), which handles these requests under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Android: Google Maps (Maps SDK for Android). While the map loads, Google LLC (USA) receives: IP address; the device's OS version, model and brand; the map library version; a pseudonymous identifier specific to the library; crash reports from inside the library; and map interactions such as panning and zooming. Google uses these to maintain and improve its service and handles them under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Map requests do not include your location, flights or name; the app does not ask for location permission. Routes and airports are drawn on the map on your device. Because the map providers are in the USA, this data is transferred abroad (GDPR Art. 46/49; KVKK Art. 9). If there is no internet or the map fails to load, the app switches to the drawn map on your device and makes no map requests.

App files. If you open the web version from an address or it updates, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages, GitHub Inc., USA) may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, requested file). These serve security and operations; retention follows GitHub's privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We have no access to these logs. In the Android and iOS apps the app files are inside the package and nothing is downloaded at launch.

Fonts, sounds, the drawn map and the airport data ship with the app; they are not loaded from outside services such as Google Fonts.

5. Share card and notifications

  • The share card is rendered as an image on your device. If you choose to share it, it goes to the app you pick; we do not see it.
  • The web version sends no notifications and asks for no notification permission. In the Android and iOS apps, the landing notification is a local notification scheduled on your device; we run no server for it. Permission is asked with context on your first departure. If you decline, the app works the same.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

  • To delete everything: Settings > Ground services > "Erase all my data". This clears IndexedDB, localStorage and the app cache and returns the app to its first-run state.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes the data from the device. In the browser version, the browser's "clear site data" does the same.
  • Export: you can export your data as JSON or CSV from the Logbook and Settings; this also covers data portability.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the equivalent rights, including to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, to ask for correction or deletion, and to claim compensation for unlawful processing. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, kvkk.gov.tr).

Because your data is on your device, you can exercise most of these rights yourself in the app (view, export, delete). For any other request, write to umutercan550@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. We will not ask for more information than needed to confirm who you are.

8. Automated decisions and profiling

None. The app makes no automated decisions about you and does not profile you.

9. Children

Albatros is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not a children's (Kids) app. Because it sends no personal data to us, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. A parent can remove the app's data on a child's device with "Erase all my data" above.

10. Security

Data sits in the device's own app storage; access is limited by your device lock and operating system protections. Downloading the app files is encrypted with HTTPS. If you share your device, we suggest using a screen lock.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will update its date. If a significant change is made, such as collecting any data, we will say so in the app with the new version and ask for your consent where required.
